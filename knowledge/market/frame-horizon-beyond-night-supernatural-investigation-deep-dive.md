## Doctrine candidate: supernatural child-witness investigation

A non-creature long-form thriller can use supernatural perception as evidence-pressure rather than power fantasy when the chain is:

1. Ordinary relational wound first: bereavement, relocation, or family disruption precedes the supernatural mystery.
2. Care before capability: an adult demonstrates repeated physical care before asking what the child knows.
3. Knowledge as danger: the child names something socially or criminally dangerous, making perception a liability.
4. Competing explanations: trauma, fraud, spirituality, and evidence remain in conflict long enough to preserve mystery.
5. Procedural conversion: an adult turns visions/memories into testable routes, locations, photographs, documents, or witness accounts.
6. Institutional resistance: local power, family influence, or fear prevents the evidence from becoming justice immediately.
7. Child exposure climax: the child is forced into public identification or physical danger, making the cost of knowledge visceral.
8. Confession over spectacle: the final explanatory payoff is relational/moral truth, not simply defeating a villain.
9. Parent-child re-stabilization: the ending proves ordinary safety after supernatural usefulness; the child is valued beyond what they know.

This overlaps the child-power obsolescence doctrine but differs in emphasis. In Evening Star, power substitutes for missing safety and should be destabilized by belonging. Here, supernatural memory functions as witness pressure inside a crime investigation: it must be constrained by procedural validation and adult protection.

## Production cautions

- Do not let “child knows secret” become a shortcut that bypasses evidence. The stronger mechanism is supernatural clue -> adult skepticism -> physical/procedural verification -> social consequence.
- Do not use the child only as an oracle device. Care scenes must exist before and after information extraction.
- Do not make the action climax purely military competence. Ray's competence matters because it protects a vulnerable witness, not because it replaces the mystery.
- Do not copy the thumbnail/content mismatch as a rule. Treat it as evidence that packaging and retention engines can diverge; future testing should compare accurate child-witness thumbnails against generic action thumbnails.
- Long slow dialogue stretches are tolerable when they rotate family care, epistemological conflict, procedural evidence, and social threat; they are weaker when they repeat the same explanatory position.

## Integration implications

- Add or refine a validator for supernatural child-witness investigation stories: care-before-knowledge, knowledge-as-danger, competing-explanation, procedural conversion, institutional resistance, child exposure cost, confession/moral-truth payoff, post-mystery ordinary safety.
- Dialogue doctrine addendum: investigation dialogue should not merely explain clues; it should force adults to choose whether they believe the child, protect the child, or preserve local order.
- Packaging doctrine addendum: record thumbnail/content divergence as a separate packaging variable rather than treating thumbnail accuracy as universally necessary.
