## Format doctrine

1. **A mid-series episode still needs a local closed loop.** Episode 11 works because mine/goblins/supply roads/princess survival form a complete mission even inside a larger succession arc.
2. **Compilation packaging is a different promise, not merely concatenation.** “Full fantasy movie/war/orcs invaded” sells total closure; “Episode 21” sells continuation to already-converted viewers.
3. **Recap is weaker than re-objectivizing.** Do not only recap prior plot; restate the present episode’s tangible object (roads, captive, weapon, door, witness, cure) through action.
4. **Journey episodes risk procedural drift.** Episode 21’s riding, directions, landmarks, and Oracle instructions are coherent but less immediately compressed than a mine siege/rescue.
5. **Long arcs need an early relational question.** Episode 11 introduces teamwork/status/succession; late episodes should likewise make one relationship or loyalty turn legible without prior knowledge.
6. **Low views on a new episode are weak evidence.** Episode 21 is very recent; age-adjusted performance is promising but cannot be compared directly with older episodes as final failure.
