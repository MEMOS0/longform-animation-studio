### Short-format lesson

## 15–30 minute standalone doctrine

1. **Short films need one arena object.** Court/ball/car/live stream; crashed plane/island ecology; or base/guardian. The strongest hit makes every object serve one moral contest.
2. **A compressed social reversal can beat creature spectacle.** The wheelchair hit creates insult → public proof → skill contest → wager → sponsor consequence in 13 minutes.
3. **Creature shorts work best as ecological behavior chains.** Insect island is strongest when threat, helper species, webs, climbing, scavenging, and aircraft tools form cause-effect survival behavior.
4. **Epic scale is dangerous in short runtime.** If military hardware, alien lore, guardian myth, and multiple survivor groups all need setup, 28 minutes becomes too short for emotional attachment but too long for a single compressed turn.
5. **Dialogue density is not quality by itself.** The hit uses dialogue for power turns; the insect film uses visual action. What matters is whether each minute produces a reversible problem, not whether it is talky.
6. **Institutional consequence is easier to compress than cosmic consequence.** Sponsor withdrawal is immediate and legible; alien invasion resolution requires lore and scale that short runtime cannot easily cash out.
7. **Low performance does not imply weak craft.** The low epic is visually coherent; do not use it as a failure oracle.
