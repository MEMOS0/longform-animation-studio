## Swapped-child doctrine

1. **The swap must steal a concrete future.** Estate, inheritance, luck, food, class safety, or family status—not abstract “destiny.”
2. **Supernatural luck needs a love condition.** “Taken by force it backfires; truly loved it works” turns the child into a moral test rather than a magic object.
3. **Identity needs proof objects.** Temple marks, rings, records, witnesses, and physical resemblance let truth be uncovered procedurally.
4. **The false child must remain a child.** Adult manipulation can be monstrous without making a six-year-old innately evil.
5. **Birth-family coercion must become active blackmail.** Secrets, threats, rumors, and exploitation create present-tense conflict after the initial swap.
6. **The loving parent must build safety structures.** “No secrets; tell me” is stronger than repeated accidental rescue.
7. **Do not treat both children as replaceable objects.** “Both my children; I love you the same” creates morally differentiated family repair.
8. **End in secure ordinary belonging.** Rank restoration alone is thin; the ending must show safe attachment, home, protection, and ordinary childhood.

## Production integration

Use `swapped_child_identity_integrity` when a premise involves swapped/secret babies, real/false heirs, class-status child identity, or luck/blessing attached to a child. It warns when the swap lacks material motive, luck lacks rules/cost, abuse is generic, identity has no proof object, the false child is simplified into evil, coercion stays passive, truth is coincidental, or the ending restores rank without secure belonging.
