## Core doctrine: protective kinship is built through material care, child agency, and consent-repaired instrumental marriage

1. **A monster reputation can be reframed as evidence of resistance.** Sarah is called cruel because she hit back; the original abuse must be shown, not merely asserted.
2. **Neglect must be material before it is emotional.** Thin clothing, split shoes, bruises, empty wardrobes, cold rooms, broken lanterns, missing inheritances, and ledger entries make care failure undeniable.
3. **Protection includes teaching agency.** The caregiver is strongest when she teaches children to report harm, refuse false apology, defend themselves, seek help, and later use power responsibly.
4. **Domestic governance can be heroic competence.** Hot water, blankets, keys, accounts, audits, food, room assignments, and boundaries are the mechanism by which care becomes real.
5. **Family rhetoric must be tested against material behavior.** “We're family” fails when the same people starve children and rob inheritances. Kinship is proven by protection, not blood.
6. **Male authority cannot simply validate the heroine.** Caelan becomes credible because he listens to children, audits before punishing, divides the house, transfers material freedom, and admits instrumental use.
7. **Instrumental marriage requires explicit consent repair.** Choosing someone because they are useful is betrayal unless cost is named, autonomy is resourced, exit remains possible, and the partner chooses again with full knowledge.
8. **Romance grows through domestic reliability.** Cooking, sitting beside rather than above, shared childcare, waiting, permission, humor, and ordinary morning care matter more than courtly declarations.
9. **Child power must not exist only to make adults fight over it.** Mila's healing flame matters because it heals, exposes corruption, and expresses trust; the plot rejects her conversion into realm property.
10. **The second antagonist should test the family after it forms.** Once the household is repaired, the crown/prince threatens the child as an asset, forcing the new kinship unit to defend its values publicly.
11. **Birth family does not deserve automatic reconciliation.** The boundary is clean because past abuse is concrete and current contact is self-interested.
12. **Closure is durable chosen-family ordinariness.** Time-jump proof, children leaving by choice, wedding renewal, breakfast, rain, and nighttime labor demonstrate that care persisted after spectacle ended.

## Craft risks and counterexamples

- The opening is very narration-dense (185 WPM in minutes 0-5). It works because objects and concrete harms arrive quickly; copying the density without object evidence would become exposition.
- Sarah's threat to sever Corvin's wing tendons is extreme. It functions as dragon-world deterrence but must not become a generic endorsement of cruelty toward children.
- The central romance begins with instrumental use. The film survives because exploitation is confessed, materially repaired, and re-chosen; without the keys/freedom/thorn-branch sequence it would be ethically thin.
- The final battle is spectacular but less important than the child-ownership doctrine. Do not copy dragon CGI scale without the legal/philosophical stake.
- The epilogue is long. Its value is durability proof, not plot necessity; compressed versions should retain selected ordinary-care evidence rather than adding more events.
