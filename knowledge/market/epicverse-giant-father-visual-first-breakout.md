## Production mechanism

This film demonstrates a visual-first version of the protective-counterforce contract: pursuit of a mother/child, then discovery of a feared giant whose repeated behavior rewrites scale into safety. The hook is not merely "giant"; it is **giant power kneeling, feeding, warming, and becoming vulnerable**. The mechanism transfers to creature-economic melodrama: spectacle must be converted into care behavior before monetization or lore.

Important correction to simplistic duration rules: this is only 11.7 minutes, yet carries a complete emotional contract because it compresses through action and images rather than exposition. Long-form versions should not pad this structure; they should multiply proof, cost, social consequences, and spatial transformations.

## Failure/counterexample value

Because the video lacks accessible subtitles, its success cannot be attributed to dialogue craft. It is evidence that silent visual causality can carry a short breakout. It does not prove dialogue is unimportant in 40-120 minute films; it proves that every scene should remain intelligible if muted.
