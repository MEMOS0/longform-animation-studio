## Production doctrine: self-selected athlete/performance romance

Use this for sports, dance, music, esports, martial arts, or any scored performance romance:

1. Turn public humiliation into immediate boundary behavior, not merely delayed revenge.
2. Explicitly reject trophy/bet framing: the person is already choosing and cannot be won as property.
3. Make competence procedural and visible: training, tryouts, scoring, coaching, selection, incident reports, medical clearance.
4. Give sabotage/injury institutional consequences that survive apology.
5. Treat both partners' ambitions as parallel stakes; neither career should be casually sacrificed for romance.
6. Give injury/recovery real time, cost, rehabilitation, and uncertainty.
7. Let the partner support without controlling, rescuing, or becoming an excuse.
8. Let rivals confess/apologize, but preserve reports, suspension, or consequences.
9. Decide the climax through visible scored skill rather than explanation.
10. End with self-selection first and romance second: “I picked me,” then the partner may keep up.

This doctrine is encoded in `studio/self_selected_athlete_romance_integrity.py` as transparent creative warnings, not performance prediction.
