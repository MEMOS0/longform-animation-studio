## Medium comparator compressed map — `CiEfupibVy8`

The film opens in a period forced-marriage/warlord household and quickly becomes a transmigration/science romance. Thought-hearing is present but less singular: around 24–33m characters fear that thoughts are heard, clear their minds, and test who has access; at 41m outsiders suspect the couple can hear her thoughts; at 100m confirmation becomes emotionally dangerous. Much of the middle is carried by anachronistic knowledge, medicine, food, research, and household/military politics. At 110m the heroine asks to join the research institute and transmit what she knows. The ending supports her scientific dream: the child is told she is “not just your mother… she’s a scientist,” and the family must support her.

Key difference: thought-hearing is one suspicion system inside a broader knowledge-competence romance. Its strongest ending doctrine is independent vocation support.

## Low comparator compressed map — `VomDIP4eI_I`

The film opens with hostile divorce coercion and allegations of forced marriage. Around 7m the husband/family can hear the heroine’s thoughts and need to verify whether her claims are true. Around 20–22m another character realizes he may be hearing her inner thoughts and resolves to prevent foreseen harm. The middle uses auctions, family illegitimacy, mastermind exposure, attacks, and rescues. At 76m she challenges the claim: if you can hear my thoughts, say what I am thinking. At the end villains receive punishment, family peace returns, and the male lead transfers property/shares, joking that he will work for her for life.

This is a strong counterexample: explicit power verification and consequences exist. Observable risks are ethical/structural: the opening’s coercion allegations are not cleanly repaired, thought-hearing often serves plot exposition, and the ending substitutes wealth/control transfer for carefully dramatized consent repair.

## Thumbnail intelligence

All three thumbnails use glossy romance triptychs with kiss imagery and duplicated/alternative partners. None clearly visualizes the actual epistemic mechanism: no thought bubble, overheard interior text, silence-versus-voice contrast, or reaction shot showing secret knowledge. The fresh-hit thumbnail does at least encode maid/CEO class contrast and a jealous/duplicate male presence; the medium and low thumbnails are more generic kiss triangles.

Reusable rule: epistemic-intimacy packaging should show the information asymmetry — a visible thought fragment, one character reacting to unspoken knowledge, or silence being broken — rather than only attractive couples kissing. This is visual-mechanism analysis, not CTR proof.

## Doctrine extracted — epistemic intimacy must create risk, not omniscience

1. Define access rules early: who hears whom, when, how reliably, and what cannot be heard.
2. Use interior/exterior contradiction: mute/cold/controlled exterior versus noisy/vulnerable interior.
3. Make knowledge dangerous: suspicion, testing, exposure, privacy violation, or retaliation.
4. Give the protagonist competence independent of mind-reading: labor, cooking, medicine, science, investigation, negotiation, protection.
5. Let care be behavioral, not merely overheard affection.
6. Preserve consent/privacy ethics: disclosure, testing, uncertainty, and boundaries matter.
7. Do not reduce love to capture, ownership, or asset transfer.
8. End with chosen proximity plus independent life/vocation/family repair.
