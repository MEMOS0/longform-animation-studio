## Core doctrine: taboo-family dramatic-irony engine

A durable forbidden-family romance engine requires:

1. **Compounded impossibility in the first 2 minutes** — wrong partner, instant commitment, age/power gap, family/friend relation, and hidden identity must stack quickly.
2. **Audience superior knowledge** — the viewer must understand the relational geometry before at least one major character.
3. **A protective misdirector** — a friend/family member actively tries to protect the protagonist but accidentally deepens the secret.
4. **Rotating social arenas** — mansion, boutique, campus, arena, breakfast table, party; each space tests a different power relation.
5. **Public shame as the real wound** — wealth fantasy alone is thin; sexual/class humiliation creates moral debt.
6. **Power must acquire rules** — protective power is most sympathetic when constrained by explicit rules/procedure, not only annihilation.
7. **Truth must threaten attachment before status can become fantasy** — the heroine must be able to reject the powerful man.
8. **Final reveal should transfer conflict from romance to family order** — the strongest hook is relational reclassification, not another villain.

## Anti-patterns this film exposes

- Do not copy intoxicated/mistaken marriage as uncomplicated romance.
- Do not treat jealousy surveillance, physical carrying, or cutting off a rival's internet as proof of love.
- Do not use mafia retaliation as consequence-free procedural justice.
- Do not let expensive gifts substitute for consent repair or truth-telling.
- Do not postpone the protagonist's knowledge so long that she becomes passive; here Lily's late recognition works because Chloe's ignorance and schemes carry the irony.

## Production transfer rules

Use the mechanism, not the plot:

- Build an original impossible-relation stack in the first 120 seconds.
- Give the audience a diagrammable secret: who knows what, about whom, and when.
- Make the best friend/family member an active misdirected protector.
