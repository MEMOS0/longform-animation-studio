## Doctrine extracted — faith/meaning-system restoration must remain practical

A faith-centered or meaning-system restoration story works best when:

1. the opening wound is materially specific: rent, food, work rejection, debt, not vague unhappiness;
2. belief is costly and behavioral: prayer, service, discipline, endurance, honesty, or community obligation;
3. the turning point instructs practical agency: inventory skills, start small, do one concrete job today;
4. the first opportunity is countable and visually executable;
5. success scales through competence and operations, not only miracle;
6. social repair requires recognition of the moral wrong, not just applause for new wealth;
7. the ending protects livelihood, dignity, and communal participation rather than reducing restoration to romance or spectacle.

Do not copy the literal Christian/Nigerian/food-business package. The reusable mechanism is transcendent endurance joined to practical economic agency. In a secular or fantasy story, the same role can be played by a philosophy, craft discipline, ancestral code, communal vow, or ethical tradition.

## Anti-overfit cautions

- This channel has only two visible long-form videos; evidence is preliminary.
- The hit’s direct testimony/monologue ending fits an inspirational faith audience but may feel didactic in a secular suspense or fantasy format.
- The lower comparator is polished and structurally coherent; do not classify it as weak writing.
- Slow cutting/static-image production did not prevent breakout discovery; story clarity and transformation legibility can outweigh motion density.
