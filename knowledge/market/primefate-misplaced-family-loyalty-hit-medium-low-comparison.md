## Production doctrine

1. Show rejected kin competence/care before exile; do not merely assert worth.
2. Favoritism must be enacted through concrete asymmetric acts: planted evidence, unequal emergency care, project transfer, public disbelief, basement/exile.
3. The manipulator's power should depend on information asymmetry and family credulity.
4. Walk-away/disowning must be a decisive boundary, not one more argument.
5. Exposure needs evidence and material consequences: files, records, witnesses, investigation, bankruptcy, prison, company collapse.
6. The family must name its exact failure; vague regret is weaker than “we believed the lies / sent him to the basement / treated the favorite as the only son.”
7. Endings need protection, chosen family, identity, moral choice, or repair beyond annihilation. The hit's protective second chance is more expansive than the medium's revenge closure and the low film's cosmic punishment.
