## Strategic implications

1. **Compilation/franchise effects are cross-market.** Veylorn’s 10M compilation after lower-performing parts mirrors RIFTBORN. Completeness can multiply existing serial demand.
2. **Tiny-channel fantasy does exist.** Drakenhart and VNDL are more useful for discovering early demand than large established channels.
3. **EchoDrama is the cleanest low-base melodrama breakout.** It should be compared against its own low-performing surrounding uploads to isolate topic/packaging effects.
4. **Everflame/Veylorn duplicate-title situation must not be naively merged.** It may be shared distribution, reupload, localization, franchise licensing, or metadata coincidence. Treat as a network/reupload question.
5. **Neural Pictures is a different evidence class.** It validates broad high-concept demand but cannot teach us how an unknown channel breaks out.
