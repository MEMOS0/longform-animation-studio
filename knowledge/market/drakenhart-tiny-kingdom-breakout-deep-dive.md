## Why this compressed 29.5-minute format works

1. **Premise paid early:** tiny city visible within five minutes.
2. **Competence before trust:** the arch/tunnel save proves usefulness before emotional acceptance.
3. **Scale has economic meaning:** junk becomes roofs, infrastructure, vehicles, tools, and community materials.
4. **Protagonist has institutional guilt:** her job signs places away; helping requires defying her role.
5. **Community distrust is rational:** giants have flooded them before; kindness does not erase history.
6. **Elder mirror:** Elias shows what long-term restitution looks like.
7. **Proof object chain:** lens, map, caves, steam vents, and drilling evidence connect the two worlds.
8. **Non-military climax:** evidence persuades the drilling crew to stop rather than ending in a generic battle.
9. **Belonging is repeated:** the ending spans years of rebuilding, not one heroic afternoon.
10. **Muted intelligibility:** helmet light, root-city scale, bottle-cap objects, drilling machinery, map proof, and final tree/lens imagery remain readable even with sparse dialogue.

- High views do not prove retention; packaging is exceptionally warm and legible.

## Production-system doctrine candidates

1. Scale fantasy should convert ordinary objects into infrastructure and economic value, not only visual size contrast.
2. An outsider protagonist should carry institutional complicity or professional guilt, not just curiosity.
3. Community trust should be historically rational: if the outsider's group caused harm, one rescue cannot erase it.
4. Give the protagonist an early practical competence payoff that visibly protects the community.
5. Use an elder/predecessor as a mirror showing the cost and duration of repair.
6. Climaxes can use maps, infrastructure knowledge, and proof to stop extraction rather than violence.
7. End compressed community fantasy with repeated rebuilding and chosen home, not only departure after rescue.
8. Memory objects should be functional: lens guides, map proves, bottle caps build, cup/roof objects transform scale into shelter.
