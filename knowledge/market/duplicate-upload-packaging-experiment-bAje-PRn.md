## Packaging difference

The AI SweetDrama thumbnail keeps the central romance but adds a workplace/technology evidence system: office skyline, X Tech monitor and laptop, chip/CPU object, patent, contract, ID badge, ring, and executive desk. The design still sells luxury romance, but it also communicates hidden technical competence and institutional power.

The Sienna thumbnail is a warm luxury romance tableau: couple close embrace, ring box, champagne, flowers, petals, city lights. It is polished and high-contrast, but it hides the heroine's X/tech competence and makes the proposition look like interchangeable billionaire-marriage romance.

The title difference reinforces the same distinction. “Dumped Me for Being Poor” gives a legible class wound and “Married His Billionaire Boss” gives direct status inversion. “Dumped Me in Public” is also legible, but “His CEO Boss Proposed … That Very Same Day” emphasizes speed/romantic compensation more than class/expertise. This is a hypothesis about packaging legibility, not causal CTR proof.

## Why this matters for Failure Intelligence

The same audiovisual film can appear as both a hundreds-of-thousands breakout and a double-digit failure. Therefore:

1. Low discovery cannot be used as evidence of weak writing.
2. Strong story craft is not sufficient for distribution.
3. Packaging can either expose or hide the actual mechanism that differentiates the film.
4. Channel baseline, upload timing, licensing/reupload dynamics, title/thumbnail alignment, and recommendation circulation are confounded; do not attribute the gap to thumbnail alone.
5. The correct production response is mechanism-legible packaging plus differentiated creative execution, not copying the winning thumbnail.

## Reusable packaging doctrine

When a film's real engine is hidden competence inside status romance, the thumbnail should contain at least one artifact of that competence or institutional arena — laptop/system screen, patent, contract, prototype, evidence, ID badge, lab, cockpit, clinic, court document — rather than only luxury couple imagery. Romance can remain the emotional hook, but the differentiating mechanism must be visible.

Do not use this case to claim that office thumbnails always outperform romantic thumbnails. Use it as a warning that interchangeable luxury packaging can undersell the actual mechanism, especially when a nearly identical upload elsewhere is packaged around mechanism-specific objects.

This is a packaging/distribution case, not a new story validator. It updates Thumbnail Intelligence and anti-overfit doctrine. No causal CTR or retention inference is made.
