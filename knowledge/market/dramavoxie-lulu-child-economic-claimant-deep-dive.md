## Doctrine candidate: child economic claimant as truth catalyst

A secret-child billionaire melodrama becomes stronger when the child is not only adorable but economically and epistemically active:

1. The child converts invisible maternal labor into a specific debt invoice (formula, diapers, emotional damages, hardship pay).
2. Public claim creates immediate social/procedural consequences rather than private acceptance.
3. Child distrust/self-protection matters: Lulu refuses strangers, hides after danger, and can articulate what she observed.
4. Child perception may discover patterns, but adult/doctor/camera/test procedure must verify them.
5. The mother's injury remains adult and material: disbelief, false evidence, pregnancy poverty, sickness, labor, exile.
6. Wealth can answer the invoice but cannot repair betrayal; Eve explicitly rejects “simple sorry.”
7. Villains target the child because she threatens inheritance/truth, making her agency consequential.
8. Legal/economic restitution must follow: forged evidence exposed, assets recovered, positions stripped, court/lawyers involved.
9. Ending must return the child to ordinary development: kindergarten, teachers, rules, snacks, peers, family routine.
10. The child catalyzes truth but does not carry adult emotional healing alone.

## Production cautions

- Do not use child support as only a comic hook. The invoice must reveal real invisible labor and structural abandonment.
- Do not let the billionaire “paying” resolve the trust wound. Money is necessary restitution but insufficient apology.
- Do not let child intuition become magical proof. Pair it with tests, cameras, doctors, documents, or admissions.
- Do not end with family reunion alone. End with ordinary child systems and adult accountability.
- Do not copy Lulu's exact milk-money sign/invoice. Extract the mechanism: child-visible economics + procedural truth + ordinary developmental ending.
