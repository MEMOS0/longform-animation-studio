## Cross-bucket doctrine

1. **BL power romance needs a non-romantic competence engine.** Perfume formula/authorship, investigation/records, hockey tryouts, dance practice, or career survival must matter independently of desire.
2. **Power asymmetry must be named as leverage.** Billionaire/alpha, vampire purchaser, king, coach, employer, or rescuer cannot be treated as neutral because he is desirable.
3. **Rescue/payment must not become ownership.** The strongest films explicitly reject bargaining-chip logic and separate gratitude, investigation, dependence, and desire.
4. **Omegaverse/supernatural biology raises consent requirements.** Heat, scent, pheromones, pregnancy, blood, transformation, and memory injury require more negotiation, not less.
5. **Micro-consent is dramatically generative.** Small lines—move away, ask, trial run, say no, floor versus bed—create intimacy without erasing power.
6. **Care must preserve negative agency.** The cared-for person may be angry, uncertain, hungry, in pain, forgetful, or unwilling; care is not obedience training.
7. **Institutional truth needs procedure.** Police, records, medical notes, contracts, evidence, and full document sets prevent romance from solving crime by declaration.
8. **Low discovery is not failure proof.** The hockey film has polished packaging and strong consent architecture. Its lower views may reflect packaging saturation, audience targeting, release timing, or distribution—not weak craft.
