## Core finding
This is not primarily “werewolf spectacle.” The durable engine is **personhood under ownership**: a father reduced to Subject 9, a polar bear reduced to property, and a daughter whose transformation forces her to feel the monster position from inside. The film repeatedly converts creature imagery into questions of naming, memory, consent, family, and who has the right to own a body.

## Transferable doctrine
1. **A transformed parent is stronger than a generic monster.** The creature is emotionally specific because the protagonist’s goal is to recover the person inside the body.
2. **Ownership language is a powerful injustice engine.** “Subject,” “harvest,” “farm,” “testing,” and “two for the price of one” make the institution morally legible without long exposition.
3. **Naming is a proof of personhood.** “My name was Grom” works because it converts a spectacle creature into a victim with history, family, and self-interpretation.
4. **Transformation should create moral danger.** Lena liking the sound of hearts makes the serum ethically costly; power without temptation would be simpler and weaker.
5. **Creature alliances work through humility, not command.** The wolves do not follow a “man”; they respond to transformed identity and demonstrated solidarity.
6. **Recognition outranks defeat.** The climax pays off when the father remembers his daughter, not merely when the stronger monster wins.
7. **Sequel escalation should not replace the primary closure.** The alien-ship hook works only after the father/daughter recognition and creature alliance have paid off.

## Production implications
- For creature/sci-fi concepts, define who owns, names, cages, harvests, or tests the creature before designing action setpieces.
- Give each major nonhuman ally a personhood artifact: a name, memory, family relationship, moral restraint, or self-description.
- Use transformation to expose the protagonist to the oppressed position, not only to increase power.
- Sound should let creature breath, silence, chains, and spoken names carry personhood; do not bury recognition under constant score.
- End Part 1/Part 2 entries with one complete relational recognition even when the world-level threat continues.
