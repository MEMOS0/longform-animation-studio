## Medium comparator compressed map — `X1VR2CLipXk`

The medium opens with an immediate mass-casualty dilemma: orcs demand women/children after the lord burned the granary. It then escalates through resource invention (iron moss), stolen great-forge blueprints, defensive/offensive weapon adaptation, federation building, slavery abolition, and large-scale elven war. At 42m the federation declares equality and shared forge technology; at 60m the lord survives because “I must protect my people,” awakens primal-relic power, and ends by expanding the federation banner.

This is strong progression-system survival, but its mechanism is already covered by existing progression/constructive restoration doctrines. It relies more on system-resource invention and coalition spectacle than on intimate institutional vindication.

## Low comparator compressed map — `6YxpGGAGtw4`

The low opens with mate rejection, wolfless stigma, herb-theft accusation, and hidden-mark evidence. By 10m a hunter/witness is dead, signal paper burns, and protection feels like a leash. By 15–20m the protagonist seeks birth records in an elder archive and learns she was registered as lowborn Omega under sealed orders. Around 30m she rejects being “a weapon with a pulse” and refuses prophecy ownership. At 40m witnesses admit coerced confessions and are told to protect civilians rather than stand for her. At 44m+ the council brands her a relic thief, releases the damned, and warriors choose civilian protection over council lies. The final line is procedural: she will stand at every hearing and speak every name she touched.

This is a strong-craft counterexample. Observable weaknesses relative to the hit are not “bad writing” but narrower emotional centrality and heavier lore density: many factions, relics, prophecies, councils, and signals compete with the simpler child/mother/truth engine.

Reusable rule: institutional-vindication thumbnails should foreground the contested object/document plus the accused person's emotional claim; spectacle alone does not communicate the evidence engine. No CTR data is available, so this is visual-mechanism analysis, not performance proof.

## Doctrine extracted — institutional vindication through caregiving evidence

1. Start with a concrete institutional catastrophe: named object, evidence, sentence, family separation.
2. Let re-entry happen through useful care/competence, not only disguise.
3. Make the bond behaviorally earned before status or romance.
4. Treat institutional suspicion as rational procedure, not only villainy.
5. Convert care into concrete proof: medical intervention, records, testimony, preserved paper.
6. Keep the protagonist investigative and boundary-setting; protection must not become another cage.
7. Institutional reversal needs documents, witnesses, hearings, retraction, and changed conduct.
8. End with restored name/family/agency before crown, romance, or spectacle.

## Anti-overfit cautions

- The hit is the channel’s extreme outlier; one hit does not prove causality.
- The low comparator is polished and structurally intelligent.
- Long runtime alone is not the differentiator; the hit uses length for layered evidence and relational repair, not filler.
- Do not copy the literal jewel/palace/princess/tutor arrangement. Reuse the mechanism: institutional accusation answered through caregiving competence, evidence, and autonomy-preserving procedure.
