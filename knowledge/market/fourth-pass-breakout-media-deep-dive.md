## 4. Production-system implications

1. **Object must become agency.** In WUFO, egg becomes Skye and then a participant; in Nightfall, ring/apple/tea/train objects become proof, competence, family memory, and autonomy. Static MacGuffins are weaker.
2. **Kindness must convert into capability.** Maggie’s protection matters because it becomes work, room, partnership, sacrifice, and chosen kinship — not because she simply rescues Ivy once.
3. ** spectacle needs a vulnerable relational center.** Giants, dragons, courts, and battles are retention devices only when tied to a specific protector/child/creature/person chain.
4. **Institutional reversal is a distinct payoff machine.** Nightfall uses documents, trust law, fraud, pawnshop evidence, and court procedure. This differs from creature fantasy and should be modeled as legal/proof melodrama rather than generic hidden-wealth romance.
5. **Sound negative space is portable.** Both creature spectacle and intimate melodrama benefit when noise drops around the vulnerable reveal and music enters at emotional transitions rather than continuously.
6. **Title cloning contaminates market inference.** Always fingerprint content before treating same-title videos as comparable packaging tests.
