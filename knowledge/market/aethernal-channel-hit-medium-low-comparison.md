## Same-channel lessons

1. Channel size alone does not explain the hit: the same roughly 25.8k-subscriber channel has 3.6M, 529k, and 5k outcomes.
2. The strongest performer is not the longest or the most “epic.” Act II is shorter than Act I and uses ordinary village/economic grounding before full attack.
3. Sequel labeling is not automatically fatal. Act II outperforms Act I despite franchise continuity; simple franchise-label failure rules are falsified.
4. Concrete vulnerable foreground beats generic heroic branding in this channel context.
5. A beautiful creature image can still be non-narrative packaging when it advertises music rather than a dramatic arc.

## Format implications

- 15–30 minute films can break out when compression preserves causality and emotional grounding.
- Longer 30+ minute fantasy films may still perform, but length increases the need for strong middle progression rather than lore expansion.
- Teasers, soundtrack remixes, and fan-unit showcases are different audience contracts; do not compare them as if they were complete films.
