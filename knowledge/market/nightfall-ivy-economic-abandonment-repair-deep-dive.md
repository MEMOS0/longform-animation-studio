## Doctrine: economic abandonment requires mutual-aid labor plus procedural restitution

A class-abandonment romantic melodrama becomes durable when the abandoned heroine is rebuilt through work, financial literacy, communal care, and enforceable restitution rather than simply being rescued by a richer man:

1. **The abandonment strips identity infrastructure.** Money, phone, ID, ring, home, and marital status disappear together, making the wound material rather than emotional abstraction.
2. **First rescue is mutual aid, not romantic wealth.** Maggie offers broom defense, food, room, and work. This creates dignity and reciprocal obligation.
3. **Competence appears before romance/security.** Ivy notices prices, invoices, contracts, and operational inconsistencies; she is not merely a victim waiting to be chosen.
4. **The new love interest must not buy the solution.** Adrian can help, but Ivy refuses rescue that would confirm the elite world's image of her.
5. **Class bribery repeats the original wound in a new form.** The `$50,000` leave-town check echoes Brett's letter: both say she does not belong.
6. **Financial truth requires procedural chains.** Invoices, wages, power of attorney, notarization, revocation, dead-vendor signatures, bank loans, purchase timing, and court records carry the investigation.
7. **Restitution is measurable.** Debt amount, auction cancellation, monthly payments, marriage voidance, asset transfer, repayment order, and business ownership make repair concrete.
8. **Chosen family precedes final romance.** Maggie's care must be included in the future household; love that requires abandoning the rescuer would repeat the original exclusion.
9. **The final yes is conditional on belonging structure.** Ivy accepts only after declaring Maggie's place; Adrian proves commitment by expanding the family rather than possessing Ivy.
10. **The ending repairs the first space.** A woman abandoned near a café/train threshold ends with her own door/business/family; public abandonment becomes chosen belonging.

## Production cautions

- Do not let the richer love interest pay away the central problem without cost or agency; that reproduces the class logic that harmed the heroine.
- Do not treat legal/financial detail as filler. In this case the procedural specificity is the heroine's competence arc.
- Do not make the rescuer a disposable mentor. If someone provides first shelter, the ending must answer whether they are included, honored, or merely used.
- Do not sell only humiliation. The thumbnail accurately sells the wound, but the retention engine is the repair chain.
- Do not confuse wealth transfer with justice. Repayment, court outcome, ownership, business credit, and control are separate beats.
