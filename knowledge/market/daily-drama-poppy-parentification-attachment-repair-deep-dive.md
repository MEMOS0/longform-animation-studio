## Doctrine candidate: parentification-free attachment repair

A child-reunion family melodrama can use a healing child without making the child responsible for the parent's survival when the chain is:

1. Immediate contradictory hook: the parent's fixed belief (“my daughter is dead”) collides with the child's living presence and a small care demand (“eat”).
2. Legal/procedural safeguarding before custody: records, DNA, home assessment, visits, and approval delay possession even when emotional recognition is immediate.
3. Public claim without exposure exploitation: the father refuses to hide the child but still shields her from media unfairness.
4. Small repeated care beats: eating, shoes, bedtime, frog object, cooking, drawing, being on time—not one large rescue.
5. Parental grief regulation: the parent uses therapy, breathing, memory places, and adult support instead of making the child regulate him.
6. Imperfect memory allowed: dead-parent remembrance can be partial, wrong, revised, and shared; the goal is not perfect reconstruction.
7. Reliability is measurable: promises have times, call protocols, apologies, reminders, and repeated compliance.
8. Child fear is named and answered: “I thought you forgot me” receives repair, not dismissal.
9. Parentification is explicitly corrected: the child may love/help but is not responsible for curing, feeding, or emotionally parenting the adult.
10. Dead parent and living child are differentiated: the child can resemble the mother without being treated as her replacement.
11. Ending proves ordinary safety systems: teachers, uniforms, therapists, reminders, and calm coordination work after the emotional reunion.

## Production cautions

- Do not write “the child cured the grieving billionaire” as the final psychology. The stronger mechanism is that the child creates a reason to accept care, while adults/professionals carry treatment and safeguarding.
- Do not use DNA as a mere formality. Custody delay, home assessment, and public/media consequences create credible friction and protect the child.
- Do not make wealth solve grief. In this case wealth buys time, space, staff, and access, but the actual repair is behavioral reliability.
- Do not end at legal custody. Custody is permission to begin parenting; the ending must prove ordinary trust after the initial recognition high.
- Do not let cuteness replace agency. Poppy's frog language, food rituals, memory questions, fear statements, and help-seeking give her a coherent child point of view.

## Integration implications

- Add a production validator for parentification-free attachment repair: procedural custody, public claim, repeated small care, therapy/adult support, reliability protocol, parentification correction, dead-parent differentiation, ordinary safety-system ending.
- Dialogue doctrine addendum: repair dialogue should include exact future behavior (“I'll call before five”) rather than abstract promises (“I'll always be there”).
- Packaging doctrine addendum: high-status environment + small caretaking act can accurately compress wealth melodrama and attachment repair.
