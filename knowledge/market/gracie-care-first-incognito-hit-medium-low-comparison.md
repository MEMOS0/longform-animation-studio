These are substantive relationship/career beats.

Observable diffusion: the story spreads across ex-fiancé betrayal, infertility stigma, prison/family corporate intrigue, fashion plagiarism/employment, pregnancy, and mental-health misunderstanding. It lacks one repeated care-debt object as strong as the train bandaging/wolf rescue chain. The ending gives her a baby and reveals that he bought her former fashion company without discussion: “You didn't even discuss it with me… Money doesn't matter. Your happiness does.” This is emotionally warm but paternalistic; her independent studio dream is solved by purchase rather than built or negotiated.

## Doctrine extracted — care-first incognito

1. Establish a concrete material/moral objective before romance.
2. Let the protagonist perform a visible costly care act before knowing the stranger's status.
3. Convert mistaken identity into compressed dramatic irony quickly.
4. Repeat competence after the first rescue: medical skill, craft, performance, investigation, public speech, or labor.
5. Give the heroine active loyalty/agency tests, not only reactive protection.
6. Do not let wealth transfer substitute for consent, repair, or negotiated agency.
7. If coercion is used as conflict, it must be named, repaired, and converted into freely chosen commitment; otherwise the story carries an ethical structural defect even if it is commercially effective.
8. Class vindication should dignify labor/community rather than merely crown the heroine as wealth-adjacent.
9. Preserve independent vocation/family/life in the ending; wedding spectacle alone is thinner closure.

## Anti-overfit cautions

- The hit violates consent craft through Nana-threat forced marriage and never clearly apologizes for it. Do not copy that defect.
- The low comparator is structurally substantive and dialogue-rich; low discovery is not weak-writing proof.
- The medium hit is a different mechanism and has the strongest independent ending, so romance spectacle is not the only channel recipe.
- The hit is very fresh; velocity may decay or reflect packaging/external traffic rather than completion quality.
