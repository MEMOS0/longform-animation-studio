## Hit comparison: `FaP` vs `DG16`
Both outliers begin with stigma, expulsion, and an impossible bodily/identity mark:
- FaP: blue blood makes the heroine a “monster” and sacrificial object.
- DG16: Pearl is a nonhuman child whose tears/body can be exploited as treasure.

Both use ownership/personhood pressure:
- FaP: chain/blood pact, sacrifice, key/lock instrumentalization.
- DG16: “living treasure,” “creature belongs to me,” debt and legal capture.

Both end by transforming hostile space into home:
- FaP: deep sea changes from prison/sacrifice site into chosen home.
- DG16: dry/debt-bound property becomes water/garden/business/community.

The difference is audience contract:
- FaP is adult romantic-sacrifice epic: fated bride, divine husband/protector, reincarnation, blood pact, magical memory loss, mutual protection.
- DG16 is family/economic creature melodrama: grandmother/home debt, childlike creature care, food-cart competence, legal proof, ethical refusal to monetize pain.

Thus the transferable mechanism is not “mermaids” or “blue blood”; it is **stigma/body exploitation + protective bond + spatial conversion of prison/exclusion into home**. The genre surface determines whether that bond is romantic or familial/economic.

## Production doctrine additions
1. **Body-as-evidence openings work.** Blue blood, tears, feathers, salt, fur, or transformation should be visible early and immediately reinterpreted by society as curse/property/threat.
2. **A chain/pact/object should carry both intimacy and coercion.** If power binding is only lore, it is weak; if it physically punishes distance/disobedience, it creates scene-level tension.
3. **Sacrifice should evolve.** Early imposed sacrifice should become later chosen cost, then be transformed by reciprocity or a new rule (memory instead of soul).
4. **Recognition beats exposition.** Illusion battles and memory loss work when love/personhood is re-established through behavior, not merely identity facts.
5. **End with spatial home conversion.** “Prison/sea/pump/floor becomes home/garden/community” is a stronger payoff than villain defeat alone.
6. **Long vertical compilations still need relational episode engines.** Even polished 70–120m fantasy fails when it becomes undifferentiated spectacle; hits keep returning to one embodied bond under ownership/stigma pressure.
