## Core doctrine: anti-fate chosen-bond re-choice engine

A reusable fantasy-romance re-choice engine requires:

1. **Public relational betrayal before supernatural mythology** — the audience must understand the humiliation before learning lore.
2. **Concrete sacrificial labor with system value** — blood essence, healing labor, economic support, or protection must materially sustain the betrayer.
3. **Fate/destiny must function as an excuse** — the antagonist uses cosmic inevitability to avoid choice and accountability.
4. **The protagonist must refuse before the upgrade appears** — rescue cannot create agency after the fact.
5. **The alternative bond must be chosen, patient, and costly to the giver** — devotion is proven through consent-preserving behavior, not merely higher status.
6. **Care must precede romantic certainty** — healing, protection, waiting, and respect for refusal build belief before the heroine reciprocates.
7. **Late repentance must fail if it remains self-centered** — regret is not repair unless it names harm, restores loss, and relinquishes control.
8. **False love/credit should be exposed through layered evidence** — emotional testimony, embodied proof, mechanism explanation, and public procedure.
9. **Institutional consequence must answer institutional theft** — titles/resources/succession earned through exploitation should be withdrawn publicly.
10. **Closure should reclassify home and family** — the ending is strongest when sincere chosen kin replaces exploitative destiny.

## Anti-patterns and adaptation warnings

- Do not copy the literal wolf/elf/fated-mate plot; extract the mechanism of chosen devotion versus coercive destiny.
- Do not make the “better” partner automatically entitled to the heroine because he is richer, older, stronger, or more magical.
- Do not use blood sacrifice/body exploitation as decorative lore; it must carry moral consequence.
- Do not let the betrayer's suffering substitute for repair. Darius is punished because he chose power repeatedly, not because he feels bad.
- Do not collapse all complexity into species essentialism. In original work, make “fate” an ideology/system people choose to wield, not a biological excuse.
- Do not imitate Aria's transformation as pure female rivalry. The reusable core is identity theft plus stolen sacrifice, not “another woman is evil because she wants status.”
