## Within-channel mechanism evidence

### MythicTwist — hidden creature/power beats generic royalty revenge

Visible high performers:

- ~758k: baby whose thoughts are heard saves royal family.
- ~629k: betrayed dragon princess awakens.
- ~467k: heroine mistakes mighty alpha king for a dog.
- ~128k: dragon killed by duke returns as his daughter.
- ~90k: crown prince betrayal + knight commander revenge.
- ~67k: demon curses heroine with endless wealth.

Visible low performers:

- ~356: summoned to kill demon king, unaware servant is involved.
- ~2.2k: dies for wrong prince, reborn for revenge.
- ~2.2–2.6k: memory-loss duke marriage, blood-slave salvation, mother-to-orphans angel premise.

Hypothesis: this channel's audience responds more strongly when the title/package contains an immediately legible **creature/baby/hidden-power object** than when it promises generalized royal betrayal/revenge. However, low performers may differ in execution, age, packaging, upload timing, or topic saturation; this is not proof of causality.

### FANTASY MOVIE KINGDOM — creature-parent niche is repeatable; sci-fi is an outlier

The channel repeatedly uses maternal/protective monster premises:

- ~457k bear-man saves her; she returns for his stolen child.
- ~405k dragon egg theft/daughter retaliation.
- ~328k T-rex raises her son.
- ~215k giants take parents; she returns with dragons.
- ~147k princess becomes white tiger her father hunts.
- ~126k ranger saves lizard baby despite tribal threat.
- ~106k she raises monster babies others fed to the monster.

The post-WWIII sci-fi video at ~813k is larger than these but does not invalidate the niche pattern. It may show that a broad high-concept sci-fi package can temporarily outperform the channel's core creature-parent contract. The correct interpretation is segmentation, not “sci-fi beats creature fantasy.”

### Quiet Reckoning / Story Secrets / Karma Keeps Score — social-test melodrama cluster

A clear pattern emerges across separate small channels:

- hidden wealthy person pretends to be a beggar to test a fiancée;
- heroine is abandoned for feeding a beggar who is secretly wealthy;
- billionaire's daughter sits beside a humiliated maid;
- billionaire wife slaps maid and husband follows/protects her.

Mechanism: **status humiliation + morally legible kindness + hidden witness/protector + public reversal**. The child/daughter variant is especially important because a child ally makes kindness innocent rather than strategic. This closely matches the Nana beggar-disguise hit and AI Sweet class-humiliation hit from the second pass.

### LESO STUDIO — faith reversal is a distinct contract

`62vBW1_B92Y` (“She Was Tired Of Suffering Then God Changed Her Story”) has ~653k against another visible ~164k disabled-husband/maid melodrama. This is not fantasy creature demand and should not be merged into the same category. It suggests a religious/emotional audience contract around suffering, dignity, divine reversal, and caretaking. Treat as a separate vertical melodrama segment pending media analysis.

## Falsification and uncertainty

- Karma Keeps Score has only one visible upload; the lead may be a reupload, compilation, paid/embedded traffic artifact, or channel-history artifact rather than organic topic demand.
- Channel pages show only visible uploads; deleted/private videos may change baselines.
- Titles in this cluster are highly trend-jacked. Similar beggar/maid/hidden-billionaire packaging may reflect search-demand capture rather than original story strength.
- We still cannot isolate title/thumbnail/story/execution without CTR/impressions/retention.
- These metadata patterns need timestamped media analysis before becoming strong creative rules.
