## Cross-comparator doctrine

1. **Protection must reverse an earlier care debt.** The strongest versions show who protected or loved the powerless hero first. Ethan’s sisters treated him as family; Kalin’s mother becomes both rescued and rescuer; Cassell waits seven years and later participates in the audit.
2. **Kin must have independent competence.** Sisters are leaders in professions, the mother/family defend one another, the wife participates in financial audit, and veterans retain useful civic identities. Protected people should not be furniture.
3. **The threat needs material specificity.** Land seizure, pensions, workplace assault, coma, kidnapping, family murder, debt, property, and institutional expulsion are stronger than generic villainy.
4. **Hidden power needs public verification objects.** Ring, badge, signet, black card, hammer, pendant, military uniform, officials kneeling, state backup, and audit authority are visual proof systems.
5. **Procedure and violence should interleave.** The low film proves procedure alone can be strong; the hit proves spectacle plus institutional dominance travels farther. The ideal system uses force to stop immediate harm, then evidence/verification to defeat the network.
6. **Reciprocal protection is the emotional differentiator.** “Your sisters will stand in front of you” is more powerful than “the hero saves helpless women.” Family members must sometimes choose risk, care, investigation, forgiveness, or sacrifice.
7. **Taboo/harem packaging may drive attention but carries ethical and brand risk.** The hit’s non-blood-sister flirtation framing likely contributes distinct curiosity, but it also conflicts with the family-protection premise. Reuse only with explicit non-blood status, adult consent, boundaries, and no deceptive incest bait.
8. **Mythic scale is not automatically stronger than contemporary class reversal.** The medium fantasy film has bigger visual scale but less immediate social aspiration than land, hospital, celebrity, VIP cards, and family homes.
9. **Strong low performers must remain counterexamples.** The commander film is procedurally excellent. Failure Intelligence should warn against over-attributing low discovery to craft quality.

## Reusable production validator

`studio/kin_protection_power_reversal_integrity.py` now checks:

- specific loss/oath/secret duty;
- independent kin roles or competence;
- material kin threat;
- procedural evidence/verification rather than only violence;
- reciprocal kin agency;
- public power-verification objects;
- violence bounded by innocence/control;
- explicit boundaries around quasi-familial intimacy;
- antagonist network/motive tracing;
- ending restoration of family safety/truth before the next hook.

This is a creative warning system, not a performance predictor.
