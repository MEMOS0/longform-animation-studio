## Doctrine

1. **Concrete institutional decline before the gift.** Utilities, feed, vet bills, decaying infrastructure, limited cash, closure risk, or expensive animal acquisition must exist first.
2. **Animal speech must expose actionable interiority.** Itch, hunger, fear, depression, desire for fame, sunlight/pool preference, injury, loneliness, pride, or performance refusal should change what humans do.
3. **Animals are collaborators, not equipment.** Give species-specific behavior plus distinct personality: vain performer, grumpy predator, food-motivated bird, proud fighter, gentle giant.
4. **Care precedes monetization.** Scratching, feeding, rescue, health exams, transport assessment, calming, habitat adjustment, and safety protocols must prove competence before viral exploitation.
5. **Autonomy is the modern differentiator.** The strongest hit explicitly refuses forced practice. Visitors may be disappointed; animal refusal still wins.
6. **Economic revival must be procedural.** Tickets, reservations, media planners, viral videos, sponsorship, investment, contracts, visitor flow, exchange animals, supply chains, and pricing make the comeback legible.
7. **Safety limits spectacle.** Double fencing, visitor limits, surveillance, barriers, no-bait rules, and transport protocols prevent wish fulfillment from becoming negligent animal endangerment.
8. **Antagonism should attack the institution.** Sabotage, exploitative exchanges, fake contracts, roadblocks, supply interference, and competitor acquisition create better opposition than random cruelty.
9. **Thumbnail must show communication and collaboration.** Sound waves, speech bubbles, listening gestures, selfie/viral iconography, and multiple expressive animals are more premise-legible than generic epic creature scale.
10. **Low discovery can hide excellent business procedure.** `qFV7Ts_B7XQ` is numerically rigorous and ethically better than many exploitation stories, yet remains low. Treat it as a falsification of simple quality-performance mapping.
