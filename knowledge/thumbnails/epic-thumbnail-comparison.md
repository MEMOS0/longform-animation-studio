# Epic AI Films thumbnail comparison

Evidence: `thumbnails/channel_cohort_montages/epic_ai_films.jpg`, channel archive snapshot 2026-11-26. Visual inspection only; no CTR/impression data. Cohort labels use age-adjusted public views/day and are hypothesis generators, not causal proof.

## Visual pattern

Top performers are not simply the most crowded or loudest images. They usually give one readable protagonist/creature relationship:

- **The Last King / Return of the Last King:** monumental aging warrior/king faces, cold desaturated world, title text tied to franchise identity. The hero’s face carries age, pain, and hidden power; the image promises a character myth, not just battle.
- **Five Baby Dragons:** a young woman physically clutches multiple small dragons while a large hostile creature looms behind. The threat/protection scale contrast is readable instantly. Warm fire versus cold snow increases separation.
- **Mother of Dragons:** tight intimate face-to-face dragon/protagonist composition. The creature is emotionally bonded, not merely spectacle.
- **Black Dragon / Kingdom entries:** darker monster/arena compositions perform less strongly than relational protection images, though still within franchise.

Low performers are visibly different product types:

- duplicate or near-duplicate sci-fi trailers;
- generic “AI cinema revolution” montage typography;
- short tech-demo/character-consistency showcases;
- non-franchise sci-fi imagery without a relational wound or protection object.

## Mechanism hypotheses

1. **Relational creature scale contrast beats generic scale.** Small babies in the protector’s arms plus large threat behind creates immediate question: can she protect them?
2. **Face emotion matters more than monster complexity.** The audience must read fear, resolve, age, or tenderness before processing spectacle.
3. **Franchise typography helps only after emotional readability.** “Age of Dragons/Kingdoms” functions as a trust/continuity layer, not the hook itself.
4. **Off-format lows distort cohort averages.** Trailers and AI-meta shorts should be excluded when evaluating feature-film thumbnail craft.
5. **Duplicate packaging is a negative signal.** Near-identical trailer thumbnails suggest low differentiation and weak reason to click.

## Production use

For an Epic-style fantasy feature, generate at least these three distinct concepts:

- vulnerable-bond closeup: protector + baby/creature faces, threat implied;
- mythic portrait: aged/hidden-power protagonist, minimal elements, strong silhouette;
- scale triangle: protector small in foreground, vulnerable object midground, threat/kingdom scale behind.

Critique at mobile size before choosing. Do not copy Epic artwork; extract composition, emotional hierarchy, contrast, and promise mechanics.
