# Channel-specific thumbnail comparisons

Updated: 2026-11-26. Evidence: persistent channel cohort montages in `thumbnails/channel_cohort_montages/`. Visual inspection only; no CTR/impression data. These are packaging hypotheses, not causal claims.

## Epic AI Films

Hit thumbnails:

- **Age of Kingdoms orc/human closeups:** large scarred faces, snow/ice atmosphere, direct eye contact, franchise typography. Even when no baby creature is visible, relational conflict is readable through faces.
- **Age of Dragons 2:** woman/protector foreground with dragon/creature threat behind; warm fire/cool creature contrast. The image says both spectacle and personal danger.

Middle thumbnails:

- **Invasion:** clean high-concept city destruction silhouette. Readable but more diffuse/disaster-oriented.
- **Infected/bus horror and Brothers war face:** strong emotion but different genre contracts (horror/war), so they should not be pooled with creature fantasy.

Low thumbnails:

- crowned king closeup, infected sequel face, orc/fire ensemble. These are not ugly; they are less clear about vulnerable relational stakes or are continuation/part-dependent.

Mechanism hypothesis: Epic thumbnails are strongest when spectacle is attached to a readable person/creature relationship or franchise contract. Pure crown/monster/sequel iconography is weaker because it asks the viewer to care about scale or continuity before care.

## Ironcrest Pictures

Hit/middle comparison:

- **Kingdom of Dragons hit:** woman holds a small baby creature while a huge luminous wolf/dragon-like protector looms behind. This is the clearest relational scale triangle in the dataset: tiny vulnerable creature + human protector + mythic power.
- **Mother of Wolves middle hit:** woman and wolf lean over the vulnerable infant/prey object in snow. Even without a large title, the protective triangle is obvious.
- **Ghost Wing middle/off-contract:** three attractive human leads in front of aircraft/attack spectacle. Polished, but it sells war/sci-fi team action rather than creature protection.
- **Son of Wolves relative low:** hooded adult hero with two wolves and blade. Attractive franchise art, but the vulnerability gradient is weaker because the adult hero already appears powerful.

Mechanism hypothesis: the exact visual ordering matters. Ironcrest’s best packaging gives the viewer someone small to protect before giving them someone powerful to admire.

## FablePop

Hit thumbnails:

- **My New Family Completely Changed Me:** split-face transformation closeup plus family/social context. The image sells identity change and emotional secrecy.
- **150 years ago… reborn:** luxurious period clothing, blood/red accent, bold “Reborn” typography. Clear transformation/rebirth contract.
- **I Became Beautiful:** intimate pajama/bedroom vulnerability plus aspirational beauty transformation energy. Simple, readable, protagonist-centered.

Middle/low thumbnails:

- Some middle examples are visually polished but repeat similar romantic/triangle compositions.
- Low examples include crowded “boys-only island” ensemble, busy maid/political montage, and whimsical frog-prince imagery.
- Montages were regenerated with deduplicated video rows; repeated thumbnails are no longer a selection artifact.

Mechanism hypothesis: FablePop’s core contract is female transformation/rebirth/social reversal. Thumbnails work when the viewer can read “she becomes someone else” or “they underestimate her” at mobile size. Generic romantic attractiveness, crowded ensemble, or whimsical creature romance is weaker when transformation is not visually legible.

## RIFTBORN CINEMA

Hit thumbnails:

- The dominant hit is the **137-minute BEASTBOUND Season 1 compilation** with an ensemble cast and franchise logo.
- Individual episodes all use polished monster/action imagery: arena, dragon, ship, giant, siren, chains, fire, water.

Mechanism hypothesis: thumbnail craft alone does not explain the compilation’s outlier performance. The compilation sells completeness, binge value, and franchise confidence. Individual episode thumbnails often look similarly cinematic, so format and packaging are confounded. Do not conclude that ensemble art inherently beats episode art.

Low observations:

- “Episode 2 Part 1” and other fragmented episodes perform poorly despite competent monster art.
- Fragmentation may reduce perceived completeness. A partial episode label is a negative packaging signal for long-form viewers.

Production use: for serials, compile and package complete seasons as separate binge products; do not rely only on episode-by-episode discovery.

## The Next Show

Hit thumbnails:

- Sinbad, Aladdin, Jason and the Argonauts, Odyssey, Thor, and Last Dragon dominate.
- They use mythic IP-adjacent titles, golden cinematic typography, ancient worlds, monsters, treasure/caves, heroes, and scale contrast.
- The viewer immediately knows the fantasy-adventure contract.

Low thumbnails:

- dinosaur documentaries, historical documentaries, generic siege/war videos, and non-franchise educational content.
- Some are visually competent, but they are off-contract: documentary rather than mythic adventure film.

Mechanism hypothesis: The Next Show audience is not simply “fantasy viewers”; it is specifically responsive to familiar mythic adventure frames. Thumbnail success depends on recognizable story-world promise plus cinematic scale. Generic ancient history or dinosaur content does not fit the same promise even when visual quality is high.

## Cross-channel thumbnail rules

1. **Read the product before the pixels.** A complete season, a standalone film, an episode fragment, a trailer, and a documentary need different evaluation criteria.
2. **Face emotion beats spectacle only when the product is relational melodrama.** FablePop/Nov-style transformation needs faces and emotional reversal; mythic adventure can prioritize world/scale if the hero/quest silhouette is clear.
3. **Creature vulnerability is different from creature threat.** Baby dragons held/protected create relational stakes; large monsters alone create spectacle stakes.
4. **Fragment labels are risky.** “Part 1,” episode fragments, trailers, and meta demos weaken long-form click value unless the channel is explicitly serial-first.
5. **Franchise typography is a trust layer, not the hook.** “Age of Dragons,” “Beastbound,” or “Aladdin” helps recognition, but the visual still needs a concrete question.
6. **Off-format lows must be separated.** Do not let trailers, documentaries, AI demos, or shorts contaminate feature-film thumbnail conclusions.

## Required workflow for future thumbnails

Before designing, select the correct reference class:

- transformation/rebirth melodrama: FablePop/Nov references;
- vulnerable creature protection: Ironcrest/Epic references;
- mythic adventure scale: The Next Show references;
- complete-season franchise binge: RIFTBORN references;
- disaster/sci-fi: use cautiously because channel fit is highly dependent.

Generate at least three original concepts per film and critique them at mobile size. Never copy reference artwork; extract compositional and psychological mechanics.
