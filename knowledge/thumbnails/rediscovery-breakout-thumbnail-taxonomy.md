# Rediscovery breakout thumbnail taxonomy

Updated: 2026-11-26. Evidence image: `thumbnails/rediscovery_breakouts_top10_montage.jpg`. Metadata/index: `thumbnails/rediscovery_breakouts_index.jsonl`. This is visual mechanism analysis, not CTR causality.

## Cross-mechanism patterns

1. **Relational scale beats isolated spectacle.** Giant father, Aethernal boy/dog/crawlers, machine/human face pairing, and alpha/wolf intimacy all make a powerful nonhuman figure socially legible through a vulnerable human or child.
2. **The vulnerable object is often small but central.** Infant, child, dog/wolf, maid on floor, beggar, or machine face anchors the image. Spectacle works when it frames care/threat rather than replacing it.
3. **Split identity works when both states are emotionally readable.** Reborn Assassin uses bruised schoolgirl versus armed glam assassin; transformation is instant even without reading the title.
4. **Class-test thumbnails show confrontation rather than generic wealth.** Beggar restrained at engagement and maid/child/floor triangle communicate who is degraded, who witnesses, and who judges.
5. **Romance thumbnails are stronger when competence or creature identity is embedded.** Dumped/Married Boss includes laptop/X-tech/patent cues; alpha king combines couple intimacy, wolf, crown, moon, and fur rather than a generic embrace.
6. **Faith packaging is a distinct visual contract.** God Changed Her Story uses large direct text, glamorous but emotionally sincere heroine, and restaurant/status cues; it does not use creature or violence spectacle.
7. **Overcrowding remains a risk.** Billionaire Daddy contains child plus multiple glamorous women; it works because the child is central and emotionally legible, but the same trope-stack could dilute focus if the vulnerable face were smaller.

## Design rules for original thumbnail generation

- Choose one dominant relationship triangle: vulnerable figure / protector or hidden-power figure / hostile society.
- If the story uses a test object, show it or its spatial consequence: bread, floor/mat, ring, contract, patent, wolf body, machine eye.
- Do not stack every trope. One wound + one power asymmetry + one transformation promise is enough.
- Avoid exact title/copy imitation. Use these references to learn composition and expectation-setting mechanics only.
- At mobile size, the viewer should know who is vulnerable, who has power, and why the situation is morally charged.
