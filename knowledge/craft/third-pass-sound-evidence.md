## Cross-contract sound principles

1. **Power shifts should have sonic signatures.** Command timbre in domestic melodrama, flute warmth in kindness melodrama, and organic/mechanical hybridization in machine personhood are different but precise.
2. **Negative space is genre-specific but portable.** Domestic awkwardness, outdoor kindness pauses, and vulnerable sand/breath silence all let the audience feel before music tells them what to feel.
3. **Music should mark transformation, not decorate duration.** The strongest clips use sparse or minimal score and let environmental/foley detail carry reality.
4. **Sound must distinguish status worlds.** Luxury interiors are quiet enough for command and humiliation; street kindness has birds/chimes/open air; machine survival has sand, breath, hydraulics, and metal.
