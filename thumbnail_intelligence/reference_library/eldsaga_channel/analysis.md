# ELDSAGA thumbnail comparison

Evidence caveat: visual analysis only; no CTR or impressions. Views/day estimates are in `manifest.json`.

## Hit packaging
- `KFds9-Rmey4` (original Wolf Father, 42.5k views/day): clean snow negative space, one red-clad daughter crouched in protection/vulnerability, one giant wolf father directly behind her, crashed helicopter/soldiers as secondary context. The relational triangle is readable before the franchise title. This is the clearest family-creature thumbnail in the set.
- `bUfsuiuV2D0` (Wolf Father 2, 54.2k views/day): much busier, but it escalates the proven franchise with visible transformation (daughter with wolf claws/fur), collar-tagged wolf/bear allies, alien ship, and enemy creatures. The father/daughter emotional relationship is less pure than the original, yet transformation plus franchise recognition supplies novelty.

## Mid/low packaging
- `IzQXfmv-wwU` (Wolf Father 3, 1.45k/day): two large creatures fight while the woman kneels between them. It reads as monster combat more than family rescue; the relational differentiator is diluted.
- `1l6K9iWJ238` (Wolf Father 4, 4.5k/day): striking split human/wolf heroine, but the mother twist in the title is not visually legible. Better than generic combat, weaker as family-specific packaging.
- `wKTglMNaHxY` (Dragon Twins, 128/day): crowded mother/babies/dragon/soldiers/castle montage. It contains relational elements, but no single eye/contact path dominates at mobile size.
- `yWuhOGkQg7E` (Dragon King, 947/day): woman holding dragon infants before king/fire; more legible protection triangle than Dragon Twins, but still cluttered and familiar.
- `SdorDn7QCuc` (Wildguard, 1.19k/day): warrior woman beside armored rhino. Scale and costume are clear, but who protects whom and why it matters are not.
- `t4xRe6dvoxo` (monster swallow, 610/day): giant monster scale and small red figure are readable, but relational intent is ambiguous; danger without care/family specificity.
- `s6h_IGFqrAc` (The Last Human, 66/day): photographic crowd and couple, visually distant from creature/family spectacle and inconsistent with the channel's clearest promise. Also a 48-minute psychological-thriller outlier.

## Mechanism, not copying rule
The strongest packaging does not merely show a large creature. It makes the creature's relationship to a vulnerable human readable in one glance. Crowded spectacle can work after a franchise relationship is already established, but it is a weak substitute for relational clarity in a new premise.
